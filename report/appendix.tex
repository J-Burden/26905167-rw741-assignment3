\section{Symbols}

\noindent{\footnotesize\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{0.95}%
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.15\columnwidth}p{0.8\columnwidth}@{}}
$a_j$ & precision of class $j$ \\
$\alpha$ & complexity penalty of cost-complexity pruning \\
$\mathbf{C}$ & confusion matrix, with element $C_{jl}$ \\
$c$ & number of classes \\
$\chi^2$ & McNemar test statistic \\
$\delta(\cdot,\cdot)$ & Euclidean distance between two instances \\
$\epsilon$ & generalisation error of a random forest \\
$\eta$ & accuracy \\
$F_j$ & $F_1$ measure of class $j$ \\
$\bar{F}$ & macro-averaged $F_1$ measure \\
$G$ & Gini index of a node \\
$H$ & entropy of a node \\
$h$ & classification tree \\
$|h|$ & number of leaves of tree $h$ \\
$k$ & number of neighbours consulted \\
$n$ & number of training instances \\
$n_j$ & number of training instances of class $j$ \\
$p$ & number of features \\
$\pi_j$ & proportion of instances of class $j$ at a node \\
$q$ & number of features sampled at each split \\
$R(h)$ & weighted misclassification cost of tree $h$ \\
$r_j$ & recall of class $j$ \\
$\bar{r}$ & balanced accuracy \\
$\bar{\rho}$ & mean correlation between the trees of a forest \\
$s$ & strength of the trees of a forest \\
$\tau$ & number of trees in an ensemble \\
$u$ & number of instances correct under the first classifier only \\
$v$ & number of instances correct under the second classifier only \\
$w_j$ & instance weight of class $j$ \\
$\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}$ & instances represented as feature vectors \\
$x_j$ & value of feature $j$ of instance $\mathbf{x}$ \\
